How to use

Your Miracle Therapy routine in seven steps

No processing wait: the treatment activates with the heat of the flat iron.

  1. Cleanse

    Wash twice with the Clarifying Shampoo. Do not use conditioner.

  2. Towel dry

    Towel dry until hair is about 70–80% damp.

  3. Apply

    Apply the Keratin Treatment from ½ in (1 cm) off the scalp to the ends, then comb through.

  4. Blow dry

    Blow dry until hair is 100% dry. Start right after applying.

  5. Flat iron

    Thin sections at 410–450°F (210–230°C), 10–15 passes each. Fine or bleached hair: 380–410°F, 8–10 passes.

  6. Seal

    Spray the pH Balancing Spray evenly through the hair.

  7. Wait

    Wait 48–72 hours before the first wash, then use a sulfate-free shampoo.

Before you start: always read and follow the directions on each product label. Do a strand and patch test 24 hours before full application, and keep the treatment off the scalp. Use lower heat on fine or bleached hair. Wait at least one week after coloring and four weeks after a chemical relaxer. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, consult your doctor first.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What's in the Miracle Therapy Kit?

A Clarifying Shampoo, the Miracle Therapy Keratin Treatment (16.9 oz / 500 ml) and a pH Balancing Spray (10.1 oz / 300 ml).

Can I use it at home?

Yes. The kit is designed for both salon and at-home use, with a clear three-step system.

Which hair types is it for?

All hair types, from wavy to coily. Use less product, thinner sections and lower heat on fine or bleached hair.

Can I use it on color-treated hair?

Yes. Apply the treatment at least one week after coloring so the color has fully settled.

How is it different from Select One?

Miracle Therapy is a stronger 3-piece straightening system. Select One is a single-product thermal anti-frizz treatment that smooths while keeping more of your texture.

When can I wash my hair?

Wait 48–72 hours before the first wash, then switch to a sulfate-free shampoo to help the result last.
